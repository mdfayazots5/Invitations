## 4. Design (from the video)
Overall: a soft, illustrated "storybook" invite. Every scene is a full-screen painted background — a pastel sky fading from powder blue at the top to peach-pink at the horizon — with a faceless illustrated couple in the lower half, text in the upper third, and brass lanterns hanging into frame. Calm, gentle, feminine, no dark scenes.

### 4.4 Look
- Palette:
  - Sky top #88B8E8, sky mid #B2D7EC, horizon peach #FFD8CD, blush #F8DBD4 — section backgrounds (vertical gradient top→bottom)
  - Cream #FBF4EA — veils behind text, cards
  - Teal ink #1E6E66 — names, main headings
  - Gold-brown ink #A8743A — parent lines, small labels, dividers (also matches the printed card)
  - Crimson #B32131 — event titles, "Venue :-", "Warm Regards"
  - Navy-grey #3B4458 — dates, times, body
  - Sage #9EB59A — accents (buttons hover, small leaves)
- Gold/metal treatment: gradient #8C5A23 → #E2B765 → #A8743A for the monogram ring, dividers and scratch card; no heavy glow
- Fonts (Google Fonts): script **Great Vibes** ("Warm Regards", host & groom names in section 2); headings **Cinzel** ("NIKAH & VALIMA" letter-spaced 0.2em); body **Cormorant Garamond** italic 500 (blessing lines, parents, dates); event titles **Playfair Display** 700; Arabic **Amiri**; Urdu **Noto Nastaliq Urdu**
- Motifs & ornaments: hanging brass lanterns on thin chains, small gold crescents and stars on strings, white-flower garlands, lotus sprays, a thin gold divider with a centre lotus. Draw lanterns, crescents, dividers in code (SVG).
- Textures & lighting: soft watercolour/gouache illustration, warm sunset light, no harsh shadows; slight bloom on lantern lights.

### 4.5 Motion
- Page movement: snaps section to section, glide 1.0 s, ease-in-out; each background does a slow 3 % Ken Burns push-in while held
- How text enters: blur(8px)+opacity 0 → sharp, 0.8 s, staggered 0.4–0.5 s per line, top to bottom
- Ambient effects: lantern sway ±3°, 4 s period, out of phase; falling petals on Nikah (12 at once, 6–9 s fall); gold sparkles on Valima (8, slow float); occasional leaf drift on hero (3)
- Taps: intro curtain, scratch card, RSVP buttons, map buttons, music toggle (top-left, round, cream glass)

### 4.6 Music
- Mood / instruments / tempo: gentle, warm, nasheed-like; soft daf, santoor/oud plucks, warm string pad, wordless humming; no lyrics; 72 BPM · length 50 s · peak at ~32 s (Valima section), soft fade-out last 5 s
- Song prompt (Suno or similar): "Instrumental, gentle Islamic wedding background, soft daf rhythm, oud and santoor melody, warm string pad, wordless soft humming, peaceful and joyful, 72 BPM, no vocals lyrics, no drums kit, 50 seconds, gradual build and soft ending"
